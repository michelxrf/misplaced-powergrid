
Connect energy lines and restore power to towns in this chill puzzle game.

This game was made in about 15h of work for the [Kenney Jam 2025](https://itch.io/jam/kenney-jam-2025) and it's a thank you to Kenney for all the great free assets they make.

You can play it at [Itch.io](https://friendlygecko.itch.io/misplaced-powergrid).

# Controls
- Move around with WASD;
- Space to move up;
- CTRL to move down;
- Right click to rotate the camera;
- ESC pauses the game.
# How to play
- Drag pieces from the tray on the left to empty tiles to place them;
- Click placed pieces to rotate them;
- Use the pieces to connect powerplants and towns;
- Once all towns on the level are powered you win.
